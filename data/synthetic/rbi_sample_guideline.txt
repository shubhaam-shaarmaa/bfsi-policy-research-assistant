Guidelines on AI-Driven Credit Underwriting and Algorithmic Model Governance

1. Scope and Applicability
1.1 These guidelines apply to all Regulated Entities (REs) utilizing automated machine learning (ML) or artificial intelligence (AI) models for retail and MSME credit decisioning, scoring, and underwriting.
1.2 The objective is to ensure model interpretability, prevent systemic algorithmic bias, protect consumer data, and mandate human-in-the-loop oversight for high-value credit underwriting.

2. Governance and Board Oversight
2.1 Every RE deploying algorithmic credit models must establish a Model Risk Management Committee (MRMC) reporting directly to the Board of Directors.
2.2 The MRMC shall conduct bi-annual model stress testing and audit algorithmic drift.
2.3 The Board-approved model governance policy shall be reviewed at least once every financial year.

3. Model Risk Management and Human-in-the-Loop Thresholds
3.1 For all individual credit facilities exceeding INR 50,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Lakhs) and corporate/MSME credit facilities exceeding INR 2,00,00,000 (Rupees Two Crores), an adverse algorithmic decision (credit rejection or rating downgrade) shall be reviewed and countersigned by a human credit officer.
3.2 Regulated Entities shall maintain complete feature attribution records (e.g., SHAP or Integrated Gradients values) for a minimum statutory period of eight (8) years from the date of loan closure.
3.3 Black-box neural models lacking verifiable feature attribution documentation are strictly prohibited from determining primary eligibility for subsidized government credit schemes.

4. Customer Grievance Redressal and Adverse Action Disclosures
4.1 In the event of credit rejection driven primarily by an algorithmic scoring model, the RE shall provide the applicant with a written Reason for Adverse Action Notice within three (3) business days.
4.2 Such notice shall disclose the top four (4) key contributory financial factors without compromising proprietary model intellectual property.
4.3 Customer appeals against automated credit denials must be resolved by the designated Principal Nodal Officer within a maximum turnaround time (TAT) of fifteen (15) calendar days.
